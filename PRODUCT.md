# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Primary**: IR Plus clients — Thai corporate/enterprise stakeholders (IR managers, C-suite, marketing leads) commissioning investor-relations websites. They arrive either as new clients (guided flow) or returning clients (existing flow). Context: high-stakes decision, moderate digital fluency, strong preference for expert guidance over self-service. Job: define a website that accurately represents their company without wasting cycles on revision and miscommunication.

**Secondary**: Internal IR Plus teams — sales, project managers, executives. Context: review client submissions, manage workflow, present recommendations. Job: translate client intent into actionable design briefs with zero ambiguity.

## Product Purpose

An AI-assisted collaborative website planning platform. Clients co-create their website requirements alongside an AI that acts as an experienced IR Plus teammate — guiding, explaining, comparing options, and building confidence. Success means: fewer revision cycles, less scope creep, and clients who feel they worked with experts, not filled out a form.

## Brand Personality

**Modern · Expert · Trustworthy**

Voice: precise and confident, never patronizing. The AI speaks like the most knowledgeable person in the room who happens to have great bedside manner. Tone: calm authority. Emotional goal: confidence — the client should feel they made the right choices.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS form tools (Typeform, Google Forms energy) — makes it feel like paperwork, not expert collaboration
- Chatbot-forward UIs (visible chat bubbles, typing indicators as the primary UI) — AI should be invisible infrastructure, not a character
- Dashboard-heavy admin tools (Salesforce, HubSpot) — too transactional, not enough craft
- Trendy AI-startup aesthetics (purple gradients, glassmorphism overload, bouncing mascots as primary affordances)

## Design Principles

1. **AI is infrastructure, not interface** — guidance, recommendations, and intelligence should feel native to the flow, not bolted on as a chat panel
2. **Confidence over features** — every interaction should leave the client feeling more certain about their choice, not more overwhelmed with options
3. **Expert pace** — the system knows what to show when; progressive disclosure over information dump; trust that the user will get there
4. **Timeless over trendy** — this platform will be used for a decade; avoid the aesthetic of this moment in favor of enduring craft
5. **Show, don't explain** — live previews, concrete comparisons, and visual confirmation beat prose descriptions every time

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Thai + English bilingual interface. Reduced-motion support required for all animations. Color contrast must hold across both light (client-facing) and dark (internal) surfaces.
